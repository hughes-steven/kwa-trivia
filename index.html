    How to play

    1. Press Start game.
    2. Press Roll the dice to pick a category at random (or choose one yourself), then pick a question number.
    3. As soon as a question is opened it is marked as played with a checkmark, and stays that way until you press Reset game.
    4. Read the question aloud. When someone answers, press their choice to check it, or press Reveal answer.
    5. Go back to the categories and pick the next one.
    6. Press End game at any time to wrap up, or Reset game in the top corner to make every question available again.

    Keyboard shortcuts

    • Tab moves between buttons; Enter or Space presses them.
    • On a question: A B C D choose an answer, R reveals it.
    • Esc goes back to the previous screen.

    Played questions are remembered in this browser, so you can refresh the page or come back later and continue where you left off.
