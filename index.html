        How to play

        1. Choose the number of teams and whether you want a timer, then press Start game.
        2. Pick a category, then pick a question number. Questions you have already used are marked as answered.
        3. Read the question aloud. When a team answers, press their choice to check it, or press Reveal answer.
        4. Give a point to the team that got it right, then go back to the categories.
        5. Press End game at any time to see the final scores.

        Keyboard shortcuts

        • Tab moves between buttons; Enter or Space presses them.
        • On a question: A B C D choose an answer, R reveals it.
        • Esc goes back to the previous screen.

        Your game is saved in this browser, so you can refresh the page or come back later and continue.
