# Code of Conduct

This project exists to give people a good evening. We ask everyone who takes part,
whether by writing questions, changing code, opening issues or reviewing, to help keep
it that way.

**Be welcoming and respectful.** People of all ages, backgrounds and abilities play this
game and contribute to it. Treat everyone with courtesy, assume good intentions, and
choose words that include rather than exclude.

**Keep the content kind.** Do not add, or ask others to add, material that is political,
religious, sexual, violent, or that mocks any person or group, including on the basis of
disability, health, age, race, gender, sexuality or faith. See CONTRIBUTING.md for the
full content guidelines.

**Handle disagreement gracefully.** Explain your reasoning, listen to others, and accept
the maintainers' decision when one is needed.

**Report problems.** If something in the project or in a discussion makes you
uncomfortable, open an issue or contact KW AccessAbility through kwaccessability.ca.
Reports will be handled respectfully and in confidence.

Contributions or behaviour that go against this code may be edited, declined or removed.
